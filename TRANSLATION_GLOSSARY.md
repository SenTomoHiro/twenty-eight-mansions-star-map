# Translation Glossary / 译名表

This glossary records the project’s maintained English terminology. It favors terms that are established, source-aware, and easy to map back to the Chinese original. Proper names may retain pinyin and Chinese characters when a single English equivalent would overstate certainty.

| 中文 | Project English | Maintenance note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 二十八宿 | Twenty-Eight Mansions | “Mansions,” not “zodiac signs.” |
| 宿 | mansion | A division and cultural structure in the traditional Chinese sky. |
| 星官 | traditional Chinese asterism / asterism | Use “asterism,” not “constellation,” unless discussing a modern IAU constellation. |
| 四象 | Four Symbols | The maintained project term; “Four Emblems” may appear in cited scholarship. |
| 东方苍龙／青龙 | Eastern Azure Dragon / Azure Dragon | Keep the historical Chinese identity; do not substitute a Western constellation. |
| 北方玄武 | Northern Black Tortoise / Black Tortoise | Xuanwu 玄武 may be retained where the broader cultural figure is discussed. |
| 西方白虎 | Western White Tiger / White Tiger | — |
| 南方朱雀 | Southern Vermilion Bird / Vermilion Bird | — |
| 距星 | defining star | The reference star used for a mansion; not a claim that all periods used the same identification. |
| 北斗 | Beidou / Northern Dipper | “Big Dipper” may describe the familiar seven-star modern pattern, but Beidou is preferred for the Chinese cultural system. |
| 南斗 | Nandou / Southern Dipper | Not the same asterism as Beidou. In this project it reuses the Dou Mansion’s astronomical objects. |
| 北斗九星 | Beidou Nine Stars | Seven principal stars plus Fu 辅 and Bi 弼 in the cultural scheme; Bi has no asserted modern stellar counterpart here. |
| 南斗六星 | Nandou Six Stars | — |
| 三台 | Santai / Three Steps | A six-star asterism arranged as Upper, Middle, and Lower pairs—not three stars. |
| 上台／中台／下台 | Upper Step / Middle Step / Lower Step | Retain Chinese alongside English where space permits. |
| 星君 | Star Lord | A Daoist religious title; do not flatten to “god.” |
| 分野 | terrestrial divisions | Historical astrological geography; not modern administrative geography. |
| 神像 | deity illustration / sacred figure | “Deity illustration” describes the reviewed digital artwork; source sculptures remain identified separately. |
| 全天投影 | all-sky projection | The fixed projection is independent of observer place and time. |
| 阳城参考视角 | Yangcheng reference view | A culturally grounded product reference, not an exclusive historical claim. |

## Proper-name pattern

- Mansion labels use pinyin plus the Chinese original in English mode, for example `Jiao 角` and `Shen 参`.
- Classical titles keep a readable English title together with the Chinese original where useful, for example *Book of Jin* 晋书 and *Dongyuan ji* 洞渊集.
- When scholarly translations vary, the interface prefers pinyin plus Chinese rather than inventing an English-sounding name.
